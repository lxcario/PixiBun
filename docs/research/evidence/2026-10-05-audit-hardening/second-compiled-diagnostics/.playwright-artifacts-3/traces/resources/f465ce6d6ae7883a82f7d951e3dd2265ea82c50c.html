01 / 2.5D LIGHTING & SHADOWS

2.5D Photometric Studio & Bounded Cast Shadows

Move the little bulb and watch the sample's highlights and shadows change. The surface is estimated from the sprite's silhouette; compare the materials and shadow choices to find a look you like.

✦
Exact Euclidean Distance Field (EDT)Linear-time O(N) Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher transform inflates flat silhouettes into dome heightmaps.
✦
Sobel Tangent Normals & Bilateral SmoothingEstimates surface directions from the height field and smooths them locally. Fine details need a look of their own.
✦
Stepped OKLCH Cel-ShadingUses perceptual color coordinates for warm highlights, cool shadows and stepped tones. The final colors depend on your settings.
