## Research basis and its limits

[Paeth's 1986 raster-rotation paper](https://graphicsinterface.org/wp-content/uploads/gi1986-15.pdf) supplies the three-shear method, not a guarantee that this pixel-art adaptation preserves every small detail. [Kung and Robinson's optimistic-concurrency paper](https://www.eecs.harvard.edu/~htk/publication/1981-tods-kung-robinson.pdf) motivates staging and validating ownership before publication; PixiBun does not implement a database concurrency protocol. [Curino, Moon and Zaniolo's PRISM work](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol1/1453939.pdf) motivates explicit version/migration treatment, rather than certifying this serializer.

The [original Reoriented Normal Mapping article by Barré-Brisebois and Hill](https://blog.selfshadow.com/publications/blending-in-detail/) explains the reference construction and normalization concerns. PixiBun's additional floors/clamps and fallback are implementation choices documented from the code. Research citations establish methodological provenance; application tests and artist/device studies establish different evidence.
