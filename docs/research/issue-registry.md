| Motion quality and responsiveness | Evaluate large parts, arbitrary-angle detail, crop feedback, temporal/artwork quality and end-to-end latency on named hardware/builds. Multi-part rigs and source-rebinding formats remain proposals. |

| Production release/update/rollback | Record deployed revision, host asset retention, cache transitions and long-lived tabs; bounded local compatible-build results do not qualify the host or approve release. |
| Historical diagnostics | Retain unexplained native Chromium crashes, older-build cold controller failures and instrumented timing failures with their original verdicts. Later passing runs do not establish their cause. |
| External assertions | Keep the [TestSprite plan registry](testsprite-plan-registry.md) separate from terminal verdicts. Missing drawing prerequisites or incomplete assertions are missing coverage. The current milestone uses native verification. |
