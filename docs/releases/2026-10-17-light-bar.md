# Light bar: themed, springy and with working look controls (17 October 2026)

Source: working tree on `testsprite-s4` after `c4b2a924`. Verification is listed at the end. Other sessions' uncommitted work in the same checkout (service-worker and security hardening) is not part of this record.

## What changed

- **Look.** `src/components/studio/PixelLightBulbGizmo.tsx` drops its fixed plum-and-gold colors. The bar is a `SpringSurface` with `pb-panel` (blooming entrance, theme edge and shadow), styled in `src/components/studio/lightBar.css` from theme tokens only. The bulb, reticle, guide rings and ray follow `--accent-gold`. The bulb glass and ray take the light's tint.
- **Layout.** Each control is a stacked field (name and value over the control), so the main row fits at 1920 px with a two-row bar when the drawer is open. Fields stretch, so narrower workspaces wrap into even rows.
- **Status pill.** While the light bar is open, `StudioLayout` marks the floating status pill `data-away` and `inert`. It fades, drops 14 px and blurs out on the bloom spring, and returns when the light is applied or cancelled. Reduced motion only fades it.
- **Controls.** Compass presets (eight directions around Recenter), Height slider, Shade and Glint with a sliding highlight. **More** opens a `Collapse` drawer with Tint (six presets and a custom color), Fill, Material, Edges and Dither, plus Reset.
- **Slider motion.** `LiquidSliders` (Labs) accepts a `pixibun:liquid-glide` event carrying the old value, and the bar's ranges send it when their value changes from outside the slider, so the lens glides instead of jumping.
- **Working look controls.** The live preview session keeps `lightColor`, `ambientIntensity` and `material` (`src/lib/canvas/lightLook.ts`, `usePhotometricEvents.ts`). It seeds them from a saved effect, updates them from the live event, and saves them on Apply. In the cel-shading path of `executePhotometricPipeline`, tint colors the lit side (60%) and shadows (25%), and fill above 0.50 lifts shadows toward the base tone while fill below 0.30 sinks them. Fabric wraps the terminator, metal and gold harden it, and metal, gold and glass catch a highlight tone. Gold warms and glass lightens its shadows. Edge depth runs from 0 (no crease tone) to 2. A changed edge depth rebuilds the cached analysis during the session. Previously these controls had no effect in cel shading.
- **Fix.** Two edits in the same frame (for example Height, then a tint) no longer drop the first one.
